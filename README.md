# Digital Dark Spot
AI-assisted information gap detection platform.

## Problem
Important decisions rely on web pages that are scattered, outdated or conflicting, and people don't know what is missing.

## What it does
Add 2-6 sources (URLs or pasted text) and a research question. The app shows:
- Facts each source covers (coverage matrix)
- Dark spots: facts no source mentions
- Conflicts between sources, with exact quotes
- Freshness warnings for dated or outdated info
- A prioritized "what to verify next" checklist

Every claim is checked against the source text. Quotes that cannot be matched are marked Unverified. AI findings are potential gaps, not guaranteed facts.
